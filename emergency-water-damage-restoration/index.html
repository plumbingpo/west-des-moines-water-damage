Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • The water is from a sewer backup or looks dark and smells bad.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
