Walking downstairs into standing water is about as stressful as home ownership gets. Heavy rain that overloads the drainage, a dead sump pump, a failed water heater, a broken pipe and foundation seepage all put water downstairs, where it has to be dealt with by professionals right away before it damages the structure, grows mold and destroys what you store there. Homeowners across West Des Moines can reach West Des Moines Restoration 24/7 for emergency water removal, and our crews bring heavy extraction gear and advanced dryers so the basement is safe and dry again without delay.

Drying a basement is its own challenge: the space is below ground level, air barely circulates and dampness lingers. Between poured concrete underfoot, concrete block on every side and wet soil outside, water has nowhere to go and evaporation on its own is slow. We know those conditions well, so our technicians arrive with truck-mounted extractors for high volumes, submersible pumps for depth, and air movers and dehumidifiers placed strategically to suit a below-grade space.

We also look for the underlying reason the basement took on water, because fixing the cause helps stop the next flood. To find contributing factors, we check grading, window wells, the sump pump, penetrations through the walls and the drainage at the foundation. We then recommend long-term solutions such as a backup sump pump, an interior drainage system or waterproofing measures that protect your basement from future water intrusion.

Basement Water Removal Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Roof water should leave the downspouts no closer than 6 feet to the house foundation
  • Four times a year, check that the sump pump works by filling the pit with water
  • Clean leaves and debris out of window wells, and think about fitting covers over them
  • Install a battery backup sump pump to stay protected during power outages
  • Use hydraulic cement to close any cracks you can see in the basement floor or walls
  • Maintain exterior grading that slopes away from your foundation

Signs You Need Basement Water Removal in West Des Moines

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Any depth of water sitting across the basement slab
  • Cracks in the floor, seams between wall and floor or gaps around pipes letting water in
  • Basement air that smells of mold or stale moisture
  • Basement windows that drip or window wells that fill during rain
  • Powdery white mineral buildup, called efflorescence, across block or concrete walls
  • A sump pump that runs nonstop, does not run at all or makes unusual noises
  • Drywall gone soft and paint flaking off in a finished basement

What a Professional Brings to Basement Water Removal

Basement water removal takes specialized equipment and expertise beyond what consumer pumps and fans can accomplish. Rental units are far slower than professional submersible pumps, and only commercial drying equipment sets the airflow, temperature and humidity required to dry a space below ground, where natural evaporation barely happens. When drying is not done properly, residual water in the framing, the insulation and the concrete ends in mold and material decay.

Safety is another concern, because a flooded basement holds dangers other rooms do not. Electrocution is possible whenever water reaches or covers outlets, wiring, the panel or plugged-in appliances. Flooding can also compromise the furnace, the water heater and natural gas lines. Our technicians are trained to assess and mitigate these hazards before extraction begins, keeping your family and our crew safe throughout the restoration.
