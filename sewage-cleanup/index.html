Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. West Des Moines Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout West Des Moines, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
