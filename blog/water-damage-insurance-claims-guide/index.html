Damage That Is Often Covered

Sudden and accidental water damage is covered under the majority of standard homeowner's policies. This includes burst pipes, appliance failures (washing machines, water heaters, dishwashers), ice dam damage, accidental overflow from sinks or bathtubs, and sudden HVAC system failures. If you cannot stay in the house, temporary living expenses are typically included, as are water removal, structural dry-out, replacement materials, and the remaining restoration costs.

What's Typically NOT Covered

The usual exclusions are sewage backup unless you carry a specific endorsement, outside flooding unless you hold separate flood insurance, groundwater seeping through the foundation, slow leaks and other gradual damage, and anything traced to neglected or deferred maintenance. If you learn about the gaps ahead of time, you can close them with an endorsement or a separate policy.

Filing a Claim Step by Step

A few steps protect your claim after a water loss, the first being video and photos of all damage before any cleanup. Next, call the insurance company without delay and tell them what happened. Do what you reasonably can to keep the damage from getting worse (mitigation). Keep damaged items until the adjuster has inspected them. Get professional restoration from an IICRC-certified company. Keep all receipts related to the loss, including temporary repairs and living expenses. Attend the inspection yourself and show the adjuster every damaged area.
