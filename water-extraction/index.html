Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. West Des Moines Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout West Des Moines using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on
  • Make sure your home has working water leak sensors in critical areas
  • Program (928) 223-6508 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency

The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. This speed difference is not just about convenience, it is about preventing thousands of dollars in additional damage that occurs with every hour water stays in contact with building materials.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Sprinkler discharge that has left water on floors and contents
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
